THE PROCESS

Motion becomes a scene.
The scene becomes a guide.

  1. 01

    Recover the performance

    ExMR reconstructs body and hand motion from a dance clip.

  2. 02

    Design the camera

    Blender renders a full orbit, close approaches, pullbacks, height changes and camera roll.

  3. 03

    Generate the appearance

    MiniMax H3 uses the rendered guide and a character reference. The Reze result follows the same dynamic guide.

What is editable? Camera placement and motion belong to the 3D scene. The final character appearance is a generated 2D video; their poses, timing and unseen details can differ from the guide.
